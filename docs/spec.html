1Purpose and scope

The app is a preparation tool for the TY27 season: the HHBn competition, then Division, then the Minnesota state competition. Soldiers use it on their phones and computers in the weeks before each event. It is not used during the competitions, since competitors are not allowed phones.

  • For competitors: study doctrine by topic or from the source document, take untimed quizzes, sit a simulated timed board, practice land nav, weapons and WTBD tasks, and log workouts. All of it earns points, promotions and unlockable rewards.
  • For sponsors: see their competitor's progress, run sponsor boards, verify hands-on tasks and get a draft award or NCOER bullets at the end.
  • For senior leaders: see how every competitor in their scope is doing, review question-error reports and moderate the discussion board.

4Study and quizzes

Study library

  • Browse by topic FRAGORD: e.g. Army history, NCO Creed and Soldier's Creed, AR 670-1, AR 600-25, TCCC, land navigation, weapons, SHARP/EO, MN Guard history, chain of command.
  • Open the source document: each topic links to the official PDF (armypubs.army.mil or the MN Guard source), opening at the cited page with #page=N. Copies are not hosted, so Soldiers always see the current version.
  • Flashcards for quick review, with an audio mode that reads cards aloud and pauses for the answer, for use while running or rucking.
  • Creed and memorization trainer: NCO Creed, Soldier's Creed, Army Song, General Orders. Words disappear progressively (10%, 30%, 60%, then all). The Soldier types or recites; the app checks word by word.

Untimed quizzes

  1. Choose a topic (or mix) and a length: 10, 20 or all.
  2. Answer at your own pace. No timer.
  3. Review screen: every question shows the correct answer, the citation (e.g. AR 670-1, para 21-8b) and a link that opens the source at that page.
  4. Per question: Mark as learned (leaves normal rotation, returns occasionally as a refresher), Report an error, or Discuss this question.

Report an error opens a short form (what is wrong, optional comment). The report goes to a queue that the admin and listed leaders see, plus an email. A report is closed when the question is fixed or the report is dismissed, and the reporter is told the outcome.

Daily 10

Ten questions a day, picked for each Soldier: weighted toward questions they missed, topics where accuracy is lowest, and questions not seen recently (spaced repetition). Builds on Squad Trivia's daily challenge, made personal. Completing it keeps a streak alive. Because Guardsmen drill monthly, streaks count weekly goals (e.g. 4 of 7 days) rather than unbroken daily runs.

5Mock board

The board simulates sitting in front of a real panel: a CSM as president, with SGMs and 1SGs as members. It awards the most points of any study activity.

Flow

  1. Report in. The Soldier gives the reporting statement; the app checks the wording FRAGORD.
  2. Questions. A panel member, shown seated at the table, asks each question aloud in their own voice. The question text is hidden by default, as at a real board. Which member asks changes from question to question.
  3. Answer. The Soldier types or speaks the answer while a countdown runs FRAGORD.
  4. Feedback. After the board: score per question, format score, delivery notes, and the cited answer for anything missed.

Required answer format

"Sergeant Major, the regulation that covers wear and appearance of Army uniforms is AR 670-1."

The greeting must match the member who asked: CSM or SGM → "Sergeant Major"; 1SG → "First Sergeant".

CheckHow it is graded
Proper addressAnswer starts with the asker's correct title.
Restates the questionMost of the question's key words appear before the answer.
Correct contentKeyword match against accepted answers first; Gemini grades open-ended answers ("Explain the five steps of risk management") and returns a short reason.
Delivery (spoken only)Filler words ("um", "uh") and response time. Feedback only, not scored.

Two modes

  • Solo board: self-paced, graded automatically. Timer is checked on the server (time question sent vs. answer received) so it cannot be paused.
  • Sponsor board (linked): the Soldier faces the panel on a laptop; the sponsor joins with a 4-letter code on their phone and sees the answer key, citation, timer and Go / No-Go buttons, plus bearing and confidence scores. Both screens stay in sync. Highest points, since a leader verifies it.

AI prompts contain only the question and the answer, never the Soldier's name. If the free Gemini quota is busy, grading falls back to keyword matching.

Maps: Camp Ripley maps from the package if their markings allow use in the app FRAGORD; otherwise a public USGS topo of the same area. GeoPDF maps carry their own coordinates; a scanned map needs 3–4 control points set once by the admin.

Weapons

  • Disassembly and assembly order (M4, M17, M249, M240), plus a sponsor-timed real-world version.
  • Functions check: order the steps and state the expected result of each.
  • Malfunctions: pick the correct immediate action from a symptom, per the current TC 3-22.9 FRAGORD.
  • Zeroing: find the center of a shot group on a 25 m target and calculate the sight adjustment.
  • Range card: complete a DA 5517-style sector sketch from a scenario.

Warrior tasks and battle drills

  • 9-line MEDEVAC, SALUTE and SPOT reports, call for fire: built from a scenario and graded line by line.
  • TCCC: branching scenarios through MARCH, where the wrong order gives a worse outcome.
  • Radio procedure: prowords and format, typed or spoken.

Sponsor-verified tasks

Tasks a phone cannot check (tourniquet, MOPP, litter carry, weapon disassembly against the clock) use a sponsor checklist: the task's Go / No-Go steps with a stopwatch. The sponsor grades and the points post.

Print mode

Because competitors work without phones on competition day, the app can print practice material (land-nav worksheet, blank 9-line, board answer sheet). The sponsor enters the score afterward for points.

7Fitness log

  • Fields: type (run, ruck, lift, HIIT, swim, AFT practice, other), duration, distance, ruck weight, effort (1–10), notes, optional screenshot.
  • No wearable needed. A typed entry earns the same base points as one with a screenshot. A screenshot adds a Verified badge and +10%.
  • Screenshots from Oura, Apple Watch, Garmin, WHOOP or others: Gemini reads the numbers (duration, distance, heart rate) and pre-fills the form. The app keeps the stats and a small thumbnail; the original image is discarded.
  • AFT practice: per-event scores with a trend chart against the standard FRAGORD.
  • Guardrails: daily point cap, and leaders can flag or reverse an entry.
OPSEC: fitness screenshots often show a GPS route map. The upload screen warns Soldiers to crop maps out, and because only stats and a thumbnail are kept, a full route image is never stored.

9Leaderboard and discussion

Leaderboard

  • Filters: squad, section, company, all competitors; this week, this month, season.
  • Most improved this week board, so newer Soldiers have something to chase.
  • Squad vs. squad weekly team totals.
  • Weekly crown and season champion, carried over from Squad Trivia.

Discussion board

  • Channels: Announcements (leaders post), Board Prep, Fitness, Land Nav, Weapons & WTBDs, General.
  • Threads, replies, @mentions, reactions, pinned posts.
  • "Discuss this question" from any quiz question opens or joins its thread.
  • Leaders can hide posts (kept on record, not deleted). Anyone can report a post; reports go to the same queue as question errors.
  • No private messages. All conversation stays in the open.
  • An OPSEC reminder sits above the post box: no locations, dates, other units' details or photos with maps.

10Sponsor and leader tools

  • Roster: every competitor in scope with total points, rank, study vs. fitness points and last activity.
  • Readiness meter per competition event FRAGORD: board, fitness, land nav, weapons and others, based on recent scores.
  • Competitor detail: quiz accuracy by topic (weak areas), board history, drill scores, workouts, questions learned.
  • Verification queue: sponsor-graded tasks and flagged fitness entries.
  • Reports queue: question errors and reported posts, with status (open, fixed, dismissed).
  • Notifications: e.g. "no workout logged in 7 days", "mock board finished, 82%", new announcement.
  • Weekly summary email to leaders (optional).

11Award and NCOER drafts

A Generate draft button on a competitor's profile, for their sponsor and leaders in scope.

  • Inputs (from the app's own records): active study hours (idle time excluded), questions answered and accuracy, topics mastered, boards completed and best score, workouts and ruck miles, verified tasks, app rank reached, competition placement.
  • Award draft: citation line and justification bullets in DA 638 style, for an award level the user selects.
  • NCOER bullets: short, measurable bullets, e.g. "o completed 1,200+ board prep questions at 94% accuracy across 14 doctrinal topics; placed 1st at HHBn BWC".
Guardrails: every output is marked DRAFT for the rater or sponsor to review and edit. It uses only real numbers from the app, never invented accomplishments, and avoids comment types prohibited by DA PAM 623-3 (checked against the version in the package). All activity is logged with timestamps from day one, so drafts can describe trends ("over nine weeks"), not just totals.

12Season and admin

  • Season TY27 holds three competitions: HHBn, Division, State, each with dates, events and standards FRAGORD.
  • Competitor status: competing at HHBn, advanced to Division, advanced to State, or finished. Advancing awards a badge and unlocks that level's content.
  • Points and rank carry over the whole season.
  • Study plan: built from the competition date and the order's reference list, week by week.
  • Admin tools: bulk question import from a spreadsheet (question, choices, answer, topic, source, paragraph, page); edit point values and thresholds; manage roles, units and sponsor pairings; keep the chain of command list current (President through SMA, MN TAG, state CSM, down to company).
  • Season AAR: each Soldier's progress, the unit's weakest topics, and the most-reported question errors, for planning TY28.

14Security and OPSEC

  • Personal data: name, rank and unit only. No DoD ID, SSN, date of birth, medical or profile data.
  • No CUI in the app. Competitors are authorized to see the CUI orders, but Firebase on the free plan and commercial AI tools are not approved systems for storing or processing CUI. The FRAGORD, 34ID OPORD, Radio Operator's Handbook (FOUO), MUOS sheet and NGA 1:50K map stay out; Soldiers get them through official channels.
  • "Government agencies only" publications (TC 3-25.26, STP 21-1-SMCT, TC 3-20.31-1 and others) are used for questions, which are visible only to admin-approved accounts. The PDFs themselves are never hosted; links go to the Army Publishing Directory, which may require a CAC.
  • Camp Ripley land nav keys stay out of the app so the competition courses aren't given away. Map drills use the unmarked 1:25K GeoPDF with app-generated points.
  • Private repo and Firebase Hosting, so code and content are not public.
  • Role rules on the server: Soldiers cannot read other Soldiers' details or change their own points.
  • AI calls carry no names or personal details; App Check limits them to this app.
  • Screenshots: only stats and a thumbnail kept; map warning on upload.
  • Discussion: no private messages; hidden posts retained for review.

15Visual design

  • Default theme: Black & Gold. Black ground, gold accents, gold-shaded training tiles with black text.
  • Theme picker (from the mockups): Desert Storm (light), Arctic Electric (light), Camo Pop, Tropic Fiesta (light).
  • Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible for text, the same as Squad Trivia.
  • Readability: every color pairing meets 4.5:1 contrast; meaning never depends on color alone (each area has an icon and label).
  • Motion: rank-up, unlock and board-pass celebrations; haptics on Android; optional bugle sound, off by default. Reduced-motion settings respected.
  • Layout: phone-first, with wider layouts on computers for map drills and the board panel.
